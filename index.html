Four planning systems · one watch area

UK coverage & official sources

PlanningWatch is being designed for England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Each nation needs its own source connections and coverage checks.

No single UK-wide planning-application feed is being assumed. The renewable-project dataset below covers the UK, but local application registers and planning rules differ by nation and authority. Coverage states will show what is connected, partial or unavailable.

England

PARTIAL · API DATA INCOMPLETE

Planning Data API for national planning datasets, with direct local-authority registers where application coverage or detail is incomplete. The national planning-application dataset is explicitly incomplete and is not a reliable full local-alert feed yet.

The API is in beta. Records need to show source, last checked date and known gaps.

Scotland

PLANNED SOURCE ROUTE

Scottish Government planning policy, ePlanning Scotland, and the public application registers maintained by planning authorities.

Applications and decisions will be linked back to the relevant council register.

Wales

PLANNED SOURCE ROUTE

Welsh Government planning policy, local-authority public registers, and Planning and Environment Decisions Wales for relevant national casework and appeals.

The local planning authority remains the source for its application register and decision.

Northern Ireland

PLANNED SOURCE ROUTE

Northern Ireland Planning Portal public register, Department for Infrastructure planning policy, and council source links.

Coverage is not complete online: the full official register is not available through the public portal, and Mid Ulster uses a separate system.

Solar and wind projects · United Kingdom

CONNECTED · QUARTERLY SNAPSHOT

DESNZ’s Renewable Energy Planning Database tracks UK renewable-electricity projects from planning through construction and operation. PlanningWatch displays solar and wind projects that remain in the planning or construction pipeline; operating, refused, withdrawn and abandoned schemes are excluded from these planned-project layers.

The database is updated quarterly and covers projects from 150 kW. It is a national snapshot, not a live council application register; small projects and newly submitted applications may be absent.

Government law and planning policy

National legislation will be tracked separately from local planning records. Proposed connections include new-legislation feeds and the relevant planning-policy pages for each nation. Any alert will link to the official text and show its publication or update date.
